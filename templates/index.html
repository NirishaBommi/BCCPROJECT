SE-STN-EfficientNet Architecture

Unified pipeline designed to bypass dermoscopic alignment noise and skin phototype variance.

1. EfficientNet backbone

Provides robust feature extraction layers pretrained on ImageNet-21k, scaled for dermatological classification tasks.

2. Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) Block

Hu et al. channel-wise recalibration block. Computes global average pooling coefficients to dynamically weight informative feature channels.

3. Spatial Transformer Network (STN)

Jaderberg et al. localization network. Learns 6-parameter affine coordinates to scale, rotate, and center asymmetric lesions dynamically before pooling.

4. Hybrid FL-CE Loss Function

Combines Cross-Entropy and Focal Loss:
L = λ * L_CE + (1 - λ) * L_FL
Compensates for extreme dataset imbalance (non-BCC benign nevi vs. rare malignant BCC subtypes).
